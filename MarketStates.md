1. Trending Market (Directional Movement)


* Bullish Trend (Uptrend)
    * Higher highs and higher lows
    * Approach: Trend-following strategies (buy dips, moving averages, trendlines)

- Moving Average (MA): Price above 50/200 MA, Golden Cross (50 MA crossing above 200 MA)
- MACD: MACD line above Signal line, histogram increasing
- RSI: Above 50 (preferably 55-70), but below 80 (to avoid overbought signals)
- Fisher Transform: Positive crossover, indicating trend strength
- ATR (Average True Range): Increasing, confirming strong momentum
- Std Dev (Standard Deviation): Expanding volatility shows breakout potential

* Bearish Trend (Downtrend)
    * Lower highs and lower lows
    * Approach: Trend-following strategies (sell rallies, shorting, MA crossovers)

- Moving Average (MA): Price below 50/200 MA, Death Cross (50 MA crossing below 200 MA)
- MACD: MACD line below Signal line, histogram decreasing
- RSI: Below 50 (preferably 30-45), avoiding oversold levels
- Fisher Transform: Negative crossover, confirming bearish momentum
- ATR: Increasing, confirming strong price movement
- Std Dev: Expanding volatility (trend continuation confirmation)

2. Range-Bound Market (Consolidation)

* Sideways/Choppy Market
    * Price moves between support and resistance
    * Approach: Range-trading strategies (buy at support, sell at resistance)

- Moving Averages: 50 & 200 MA flattening, price moving between them
- MACD: MACD line close to Signal line, histogram near zero
- RSI: Between 40-60, no strong momentum
- Fisher Transform: Near zero, lacking directional movement
- Std Dev: Low values, indicating lack of volatility

* Breakout Phase
    * Price attempts to break out of the range
    * Approach: Breakout trading strategies (wait for confirmation, volume analysis)

- Moving Averages: 20 & 50 MA showing divergence (indicating potential breakout)
- MACD: Sudden increase in histogram, MACD crossing signal line
- RSI: Moving above 60 (bullish breakout) or below 40 (bearish breakout)
- ATR: Sharp increase, indicating volatility expansion
- Std Dev: Expanding, confirming breakout
- Fisher Transform: Sharp divergence, confirming strong move



3. Reversal Market


* Trend Reversal (Market Shift)
    * Change in trend direction (uptrend to downtrend or vice versa)
    * Approach: Reversal patterns (head & shoulders, double tops/bottoms)

- Moving Averages: Price breaking 200 MA with strong momentum
- MACD: Strong divergence, crossover signal
- RSI: Moving out of extreme levels (above 30 or below 70)
- Fisher Transform: Divergence from price action, early signal for reversal
- ATR: Spikes during reversal, confirming strong move
- Std Dev: Expanding, signaling volatility shift

* Pullback/Correction
    * Temporary retracement before continuation
    * Approach: Fibonacci retracement, moving average bounces


    - Moving Averages: Price temporarily touching 50 MA in an uptrend
    - MACD: Histogram decreasing but still above zero
    - RSI: Pulling back from overbought (but staying above 50)
    - Fisher Transform: Minor pullback, no extreme divergence
    - ATR: Decreasing slightly during correction phase

4. Volatility-Based States


* High Volatility (Expansion Phase)
    * Large price swings, news-driven movements
    * Approach: Volatility breakout strategies, ATR-based stop losses

    - ATR: Rising sharply
    - Std Dev: Expanding significantly
    - MACD: Strong increase in histogram size
    - RSI: Breaking 70 (bullish) or falling below 30 (bearish)


* Low Volatility (Contraction Phase)
    * Tight price movement, low volume
    * Approach: Mean reversion strategies, wait for breakout

- ATR: Very low values
- Std Dev: Contracting (volatility squeeze)
- MACD: Flat histogram, lines close together
- Bollinger Bands: Narrowing (indicating upcoming breakout)

5. Market Sentiment-Driven States


* Overbought Condition
    * RSI/Stochastic at extreme high levels
    * Approach: Look for reversal or shorting opportunities

    - RSI: Above 70
    - Fisher Transform: Peaking near extreme high levels
    - MACD: Histogram divergence (lower highs despite price rising)


* Oversold Condition
    * RSI/Stochastic at extreme low levels
    * Approach: Look for buying opportunities

- RSI: Below 30
- Fisher Transform: Near extreme low levels
- MACD: Bullish divergence (higher MACD lows despite price falling)




6. Liquidity & Order Flow Phases


* Accumulation Phase
    * Smart money entering the market at low volatility
    * Approach: Volume analysis, Wyckoff accumulation strategy

    - Moving Averages: 200 MA flattening, price stabilizing
    - MACD: Weak histogram, low momentum
    - RSI: Stays in the 40-50 range, no extreme movement
    - Volume: Increasing slightly without major price moves


* Distribution Phase
    * Smart money exiting before reversal
    * Approach: Look for topping patterns and breakdown signals

- Moving Averages: 50 & 200 MA converging
- MACD: Bearish divergence, decreasing histogram
- RSI: Moving from 70 downwards, indicating loss of momentum
- Volume: Increasing, but price struggles to move higher
